Questions

Quick answers.

I don't live in India. Can I still work with you?

Yes. Everything happens on video calls and WhatsApp, so you can join from any country. We plan your sessions around your time zone.

Do I need to meet you in person?

No. Sessions are online. If you'd like to meet, choose Signature In Person. We do it anywhere; outside Bangalore, travel and stay costs are extra.

Will you put me in a body-shape box like apple or pear?

No. We ask what you feel conscious about and what you love about your body, then tell you which necklines, sleeves, fits and lengths work for you.

Will my stylist push her own taste on me?

No. We first find your personal style, and every suggestion is checked by a panel of 5 stylists so it suits you, not us.

Do you style sarees and Indian wear?

Yes. We specialise in Indian women. We plan sarees, kurtas, lehengas and Indo-western outfits along with your work and casual wear.

Does colour analysis work for Indian skin tones?

Yes. We use a system made for Indian and dusky skin tones, and give you 30 wearable colours plus your gold, silver and makeup shades.

Do I have to buy a whole new wardrobe?

No. We start with the clothes you already have and only suggest what's missing, within the budget per piece that you set.

What if a shopping link sells out?

Tell us on WhatsApp. We send you a replacement link at no extra cost for 6 months.

I'm shy about sending photos.

That's normal. Only your stylists see them, and we tell you exactly which photos to take.

How is this different from following stylists on Instagram?

Instagram tips are for everyone. We study your body, face, colours, lifestyle and wardrobe, and give you a plan and a personalised lookbook of 150+ looks made only for you.

Is there a discount?

Yes. For our year-end offer, the first 50 clients get 10% off any package. Book your free call and confirm your package to claim it. You don't need to pay anything upfront.

What happens after I fill in the form?

You pick a time for your free 15-minute call and we send our brochure on WhatsApp. There's no payment and no pressure.
